# Coastal Property Restorations — What We Need From You to Launch the New Site

The new website is built. What's left is the real stuff only you have — answers and photos.
Nothing here needs to be polished. Voice memos, phone photos, and quick texts all work.

---

## Part 1 — Nine quick questions

1. **Rank your services by money.** Of these, which actually pay the bills, best to worst?
   Roofing / storm & water restoration / kitchens / bathrooms / flooring / anything else.

2. **Mold:** do you hold a Florida mold remediation license (MRSR)? *(Yes / No / license #)*
   We can't advertise mold work without it.

3. **Which of these do you actually offer, and who answers the phone at 2am?**
   Emergency board-up / contents cleaning / fire restoration / true 24-7 dispatch.
   If nobody answers overnight, that's fine — we just won't promise it on the site.

4. **Where are your reviews?** Google, Facebook, Angi? If you have a Google Business
   Profile, we need login access (or add us as a manager).

5. **License numbers to show on the site** — contractor license(s), and a copy/photo of
   your insurance certificate. Florida requires the license number in advertising.

6. **Do you have a financing partner** for remodels? *(Yes — who / No)*

7. **How many years in business**, and the founding story in 2–3 sentences — voice memo
   is perfect. How did the company start? Why restoration? What was Ian like for you?

8. **Confirm your cities, in order.** We're launching with Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte,
   Fort Myers. Right order? Anything missing that matters more?

9. **Who controls the domain and current website?** We need logins for the domain
   registrar (where coastalpropertyrestorations.com is registered) and current hosting.
   If you're not sure, forward any old emails/invoices from the web company.

## Part 2 — Photo homework (phone photos are perfect)

**The rule: real beats pretty.** A slightly crooked phone photo of your actual work
outsells a stock photo every time.

- [ ] **10–20 job photos** across roofing, storm/water damage, kitchens, bathrooms
- [ ] **Before/after pairs** wherever you have them — these are gold (3 pairs per
      restoration service is the target)
- [ ] **6–12 finished shots** of your best kitchen and best bathroom
- [ ] **1 photo per city** — any real job in Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Fort Myers
- [ ] **You** — a simple headshot, outside by a truck is great
- [ ] **Crew or truck** — one good shot

For your 3 most memorable jobs, also text us: what city, what happened, what you did,
roughly how long it took, and whether insurance was involved. Those become the stories
that sell better than any slogan.
