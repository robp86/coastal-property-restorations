# New Website — What We Need From You

The new site is built and waiting on the items below. Nothing here is homework you
need to sit down for — most of it is a 20-minute phone call plus forwarding some
photos from your phone.

## 1. Ten quick questions (one phone call)

1. **Rank your services by money.** Roofing, storm/water restoration, kitchens,
   bathrooms, flooring, other — which actually pay the bills, in order?
2. **Mold:** do you hold a Florida mold remediation license? (If yes, what's the
   number — if no, we keep mold off the site for now.)
3. **Which of these do you actually offer:** emergency board-up? contents cleaning?
   fire damage restoration?
4. **Who answers the phone at 2am?** You, an answering service, or nobody? (The
   site says 24/7 — it needs to be true.)
5. **Your license numbers** — contractor license, and any roofing or other
   certifications. Exact numbers; they go in the website footer (Florida requires it).
6. **Where are your reviews?** Google, Facebook, Angi? Send links if you have them.
7. **Financing:** do you have a lender or financing program for remodels? If yes, who?
8. **Cities, in order:** which towns do you want more work from, ranked?
9. **Your email address** for the website contact form — and do you want form
   leads texted to you too? (Recommended: yes.)
10. **Logins:** who has access to the domain (where the website address is
    registered), the current website hosting, and your Google Business Profile?
    We need all three before launch.

## 2. Photos (forward from your phone — no editing needed)

- **10–20 job photos** across roofing, storm/water jobs, kitchens, bathrooms.
  Before-and-after pairs are the most valuable thing you can send.
- **A photo of you** and one of the crew or a truck.
- Rough phone photos are fine. Real beats pretty — we will not use stock photos.

## 3. Three stories (2 minutes each, voice memo is fine)

Pick three jobs you're proud of and tell us: what city, what happened, what you
did, roughly how long it took, and whether insurance was involved. These become
the project stories on the site.

## 4. In your own words (talk, don't write — we'll shape it)

- How and why you started the company.
- For each main service: what a typical job looks like and what customers
  worry about most.

---

**That's everything.** Once we have this, the site launches with your real work,
your real reviews, and your real story on it — which is exactly what makes
homeowners call you instead of the next guy.
